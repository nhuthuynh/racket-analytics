## 3. Mad / Sad / Glad

**Glad (what went well)**

- **The product goal works live and fast.** 5/5 fresh-account journeys over https. Corrections p95 35.3 ms against a 1,500 ms target, reads 16.8 ms against 300 ms, and the first video frame 672.7 ms against 1,500 ms on the throttled reference profile. Undo restores a byte-identical sheet, and conflicts are kept as "needs your decision".
- **The carry-over-first plan worked.** C-01..C-05 and ST-013b were fixed red-first before new stories (A2). The 17 carried Sprint 1 families went from 17 open to 2.
- **WebKit went green on CI.** 0 Sprint 1 `[webkit]` failures since run 37438997898; the five WebKit-only Sprint 2 failures were found to be test defects and fixed (SRE-S2-07).
- **The H.264 evidence gap was closed without a PO exception.** The EM put Chrome for Testing in the evidence environment (ADR 0036), so E2E-02-04, G02-06 (c) and the V-01 axe check run on the local stack.
- **Dry-running methods paid off** (ADR 0033 rule 2). QA found 3 method defects and the EM found G02-04's seed path (SRE-S2-02) before the verifier ran. The verifier's rounds 2 and 3 found no method defect.
- **The engine stayed strong.** Oracle 100,000 / 0, rules mutation 0.8635 as a gate for the first time, golden replay byte-identical, and 70 provisional rows kept separate from Must FRs.
- **Security found real gaps early**, and the owners closed them red-first: an append-only bypass and owner TRUNCATE on the audit table (SEC-S2-TM-01/02), edge headers for media on the app origin (SEC-S2-TM-03), and footage of people kept out of git (SEC-S2-TM-06).

**Sad (what went wrong)**

- The goal still fails on findings, not on the product, for the second sprint in a row.
- The sprint report said "open 2" for three rounds; the true number was 14.
- No story is DoD-done, because the Sprint 1 merge (PR #1) is still waiting.

**Mad (what must not happen again)**

- Review round 3's findings were never written down (M1). It is the same failure as Sprint 1 M10, and it happened despite the rule written to prevent it.
- Five people were asked to decide design questions, and none of them was ever run (M2).
