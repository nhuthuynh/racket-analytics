## 4. What went well

- **Red-first worked at sprint scale.** QA's 74 red tests behind one seam contract (ADR 0012) became the BE work queue, and every implementing story turned its suite green without weakening a test. `git status` showed that no existing backend test file was edited by BE during implementation.
- **Real services, not mocks.** `scripts/dev-postgres.sh` and `scripts/dev-objectstore.sh` gave every lane real Postgres 16 and SeaweedFS without Docker. No moto fallback was needed (ADR 0008 note).
- **Hooks proved themselves and found their own bugs.** Demoing the hooks exposed a cache-key bug and an ordering bug, and both were fixed test-first (decision-log, SRE rows).
- **Adversarial review found real, serious defects:** silent data corruption (R1-01), SSRF (SEC-R1-01) and a false-passing sandbox test (QA-R2-02), all before any user saw them.
- **Evidence culture.** Almost every claim in the review rounds has a command and its output. Fixes include mutation checks (for example "without the router dependency, 6 tests fail").
- **Licence discipline.** A GPL ffmpeg was caught and replaced by a pinned, checksum-verified LGPL ffprobe with a build guard (ADR 0020). SPIKE-01 tiered weights by their training data (ADR 0015).

Round 3's minors (charset, `Tus-Resumable` on 405) are borderline over-flagging against "flag only correctness, security or requirement gaps" [EP/ENG-24] (judgment).
- **Transcripts.** The EM read the lane results and the three review-round outputs. They show strong evidence habits, and an auto-fix agent that repeatedly marked items "not my lane" without any route to the right owner [DPA/AI-05].
- **Process rules.** Bypassed: the PR size limit (M1), TDD order for infra glue (M10), and tests written in another lane that needed QA approval (logged correctly in test-change-requests.md, never decided). Not bypassed: no accepted test was weakened or deleted, and secrets were never written (hooks).

## 9. Decisions taken in this retro

- **ADR 0022** (`docs/decisions/0022-review-loop-routing-and-integration-smoke.md`): fix-loop findings are routed to their owner role, an integrated Compose + E2E smoke runs before review round 1, the commit unit is one story or PR, and threat-model controls become story acceptance criteria. `docs/process/working-agreement.md` §5 and §7 were edited accordingly (dated notes).
- **ADR 0010 recalibration (dated note, not a new ADR):** the Sprint 1 load factor stays at 80%. Each story now gets an explicit "external evidence" item (CI run, browser matrix, human or peer review) with its own owner and due date. Sprint 0's slip came entirely from such items. The measured completed ratio is 0.68 (implemented) and 0.00 (full DoD), and it feeds the Sprint 2 load-factor rule.
- **Escalation (working-agreement §7.5):** the 3-iteration limit was reached. Open blockers and majors R3-02, QA-R3-02, R3-03 and QA-R3-04 go to the human product owner in the sprint review pack (sprint-report §6).
