### Stop (what went wrong)

- Running a review round in parallel with a verification round (M1).
- Starting the build before the gates, and then building past them under goal pressure (M2, M3).
- Treating "push and dispatch CI" as nobody's step (M5).
- Adopting a merge rule (ADR 0039) without creating the branches that make it possible (M6).

- A gate phase on day 1, rounds in series, CI at every round head, ticket branches at planning (ADR 0047).
