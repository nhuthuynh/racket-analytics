## 6. Capture-guide consent line (ST-015, PD-R2R-03, flows-sprint-01 §10.1 D-8)

Wording under review: **"Film only people who agree to be filmed. Don't upload matches with anyone under 18."**

**Decision: accepted, unchanged** (security-privacy-engineer, 2026-10-06). Reasons: it is an instruction to the user, not a statement about our processing, so it promises nothing the product does not do; it discloses nothing; it makes no legal claim (no words about law, rights or compliance) until the US/AU legal review; it carries the PO-accepted OQ-05 recommendation (minors) and the OQ-06 courtesy (consent). It does not replace the age gate (Sprint 4) and must not be cited as a control that prevents uploads of minors (T-GS-3 style residual, judgment). Next step (owner senior-frontend-engineer): red test that G-01 shows the exact string as a second `notice`, then the constant in `web/src/lib/content/capture-guide.ts`.
