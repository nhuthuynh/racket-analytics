## 7. Open questions for the NFR-070 legal review (US and AU)

These are **questions**, not statements of law. The answers belong to a qualified human reviewer, routed to the PO by the EM. Until the answers come, the ADR 0006 interim windows apply (hidden ≤ 1 min, purged ≤ 7 days, abandoned uploads ≤ 24 h). There is no real-user beta in Sprint 3.

| Q-AU-2 | AU | Do the Privacy Act and the APPs apply to this product at its planned size, given the small-business exemption question? |
| Q-AU-3 | AU | Are video of identifiable people, and pose keypoints derived from it later, "sensitive information" (biometric), so that consent rules for collection apply (APP 3)? |
