## Decisions

| Date | Who | Decision | Evidence | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-12 | senior-ml-cv-engineer | **R1 compares in whole milliseconds:** `round(abs(Δ) × 1000) ≤ 1000` on the start and on the end | `test_r1_…[1.0 s with float noise: match]` (`2.1 − 1.1` is `1.0000000000000002` in floats); `…[end 1.001 s late: no match]` | Exactly 1.0 s must match (ADR 0004 "within ±1.0 s"). Raw float comparison would miss it on ordinary timestamps. Video timestamps are not finer than 1 ms at 30-240 fps |
| 2026-11-12 | senior-ml-cv-engineer | **Merge and split are explicit rules, not left to R1.** A prediction that overlaps two gold rallies which do not overlap each other is a merge; a gold rally overlapped by two predictions which do not overlap each other is a split. Either one matches nothing. Overlap is strict, so touching end to start is not an overlap | `test_r2_…[within 1 s of the first, overlaps 2]`, `test_r3_…[first piece within 1 s of both ends]`, `test_r2_touching_…`, `test_r3_touching_pieces_are_still_a_split` | ADR 0004 says "not merged or split" separately from the ±1.0 s rule. With a gap under 1 s between rallies, R1 alone would accept a merged or split prediction |
| 2026-11-12 | senior-ml-cv-engineer | **Two overlapping predictions on one gold rally are duplicates, not a split** (R4: 1 TP, 1 FP) | `test_r4_two_predictions_near_one_gold_rally_give_one_tp_and_one_fp`; scenario NFR-006.4 | NFR-006.4 names this case. A split cuts a rally into pieces, and pieces do not overlap each other |
| 2026-11-12 | senior-ml-cv-engineer | **Maximum one-to-one matching (Kuhn's augmenting paths), not greedy nearest-first** | `test_r4_the_matching_is_maximal_not_greedy`; Hypothesis property `test_property_the_matching_is_maximum` against a brute-force oracle; order-invariance property | The TP count must not depend on input order or on which pair is nearest. Which pair is chosen does not change the counts, so no distance tie-break is needed |
| 2026-11-12 | senior-ml-cv-engineer | **Zero denominators score 0.0; no predictions and no gold rallies is a `ValueError`.** `f1_pct` reports F1 in % (ADR 0060 row M11 unit); `precision`, `recall` and `f1` stay in [0, 1] | `test_no_predictions_scores_zero`, `test_no_gold_rallies_scores_zero`, `test_nothing_to_score_is_refused` | An empty gold set has nothing to measure. The ≥ 100 gold-rally minimum for an automatic verdict (ADR 0060 Rule 3) belongs to the eval runner, not to the matcher |
| 2026-11-12 | senior-ml-cv-engineer | **No integration test for this ticket** | The acceptance criterion requires "no I/O"; `test_rally_f1_purity.py` checks the imports and calls | A pure function crosses no boundary (testing-strategy: integration = Postgres, object store, queue, worker or filesystem). The cross-level check is the scenario binding (`backend/tests/features/test_rally_f1.py`) plus the infra wiring test for the nightly step (`infra/tests/test_nightly_mutation_rally_f1.py`) |
| 2026-11-12 | senior-ml-cv-engineer | **The purity check lives in its own file, outside the mutation selection** | First local mutation run: `failed to collect stats` because the mutated module imports `mutmut` | mutmut rewrites the module with its own import, so a source-text check fails under mutation for a reason unrelated to the mutants |
