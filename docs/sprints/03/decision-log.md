| 2026-10-08 | senior-frontend-engineer (CI-WEBKIT-PD-R3S2-02) | **Cause of the WebKit-only failure of `fe-minors.spec.ts:24` (PD-R3S2-02): the spec routes `/api/matches/{id}/corrections` with `page.route` but does not block service workers.** `public/sw.js` has a `fetch` handler and calls `clients.claim()`, and `receivedMatch` signs in through pages first, so by `page.goto('/matches/{id}/sheet')` the page is controlled; in WebKit its fetches then go through the worker and Playwright's route never sees them (Chromium routes them anyway).
